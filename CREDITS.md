The 3D models used in MarketVerse are provided by [Polyfork](https://polyfork.dev) under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.

Models were modified (scaled, recolored, merged) for real-time visualization, building height scaling by market capitalization, and performance optimization.
